# Contributing to Wyatt

Design suggestions and careful build reports are welcome. This release is CAD-only, and physical fit is still unverified. Use the design-feedback or build-report issue template to make evidence easy to compare.

For a design change, name the revision and exact part filename, explain the problem, and include a dimensioned sketch or small proposed source change. Keep the original geometry and attribution available. Do not silently scale, split, mirror covers or move camera/microphone openings. Describe any change to port access, sensor airflow, speaker retention or print orientation.

For a build report, include printer/nozzle/material, slicer settings, coupon results and measured component dimensions. Distinguish printed/assembled observations from a CAD proposal. Report failures as well as successes; a single build does not validate every component variant.

Do not upload provider keys, Wi-Fi passwords, tokens, calendar exports, device backups, private photos or identifiable camera captures. Crop a fit photo to the part being discussed. Existing sourcing links are references, not supplier endorsements or promises of compatibility.

Contributions to this CAD/documentation package must preserve the applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and share-alike terms. State what you changed and do not include third-party material you cannot redistribute. See [attribution](hardware/r4/ATTRIBUTION.txt).
