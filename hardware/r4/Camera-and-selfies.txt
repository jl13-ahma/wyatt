WYATT R4 / FRONT CAMERA AND SELFIE CLEARANCE / 28 OCTOBER 2026
R4 is the selected design. The original Korvo camera is retained in its original
position. The added R4 parts leave the original upper-front notch and the central
functional-front area unobstructed in the CAD review. Its forward view is a required fit check, not a decorative feature.

PRINT / FINISH REQUIREMENT
- Preserve the original manufacturer's camera opening in the central cassette.
  Keep the lens area and adjacent microphone openings clear through the new
  outer frame. Do not bridge them with leather, adhesive, paint, grille mesh,
  opaque trim or an unqualified cover-glass layer.
- Do not relocate the camera or invent a new lens hole from the appearance
  illustration. STEP/STL geometry and the real board determine its location.
- Keep finishing material inside the designated top/side wrap area. Identify
  the actual lens and camera-board position before cutting the final leather.

PHYSICAL SELFIE CHECK
1. Fit the original cassette and board to TEST_06_Cassette_Port_Lane_Coupon with
   its retainer, then use a working camera preview on that exact board, for
   example the official factory camera demo. Wyatt firmware is not included
   in this CAD-only release.
2. Check that the camera still faces the user. Compare the view before/after
   fitting the frame: a face at normal selfie distance and a well-lit grid should
   remain visible, including all image corners, without a dark edge or reflection
   caused by the frame. Check with the intended desk angle too.
3. Repeat with the complete first R4 shell, rear covers and final leather fitted.
   If an edge is clipped, revise the offending lip/material after measuring the
   actual lens location. Do not assume a manufacturer's camera FOV or lens depth
   that has not been verified for this particular module.

PRIOR BENCH CAMERA OBSERVATION / SOFTWARE NOT INCLUDED
The project owner previously confirmed forward-facing preview and an in-RAM
Snapshot freeze on the owned board during bench development. This observation
is separate from the unprinted R4 enclosure. That development app is not part
of this CAD-only release. Persistent photo saving/export/sharing is not
implemented yet. This clearance requirement reserves the hardware for selfies;
it is not a claim that a photo-storage feature or assembled-case optical test
has already passed. No camera capture, firmware change or photo upload was made
for this CAD/printing check.
