BAMBU STUDIO SETUP
1. Select the actual P1S printer, installed nozzle and build-plate profile.
   The standard 0.4 mm nozzle and a 0.20 mm layer preset are suitable starting
   choices. Use the chosen filament's own profile; ordinary PETG is supported.
2. Print the small fit coupons and their companion parts first, in the same
   material. Follow PRINT-MANIFEST.json for quantities and supplied orientation.
3. Import 01_Horizontal_Cabinet.stl alone on its plate, front face down,
   scale 100 percent. Centre it on the plate, with its 240 mm side along X.
4. A 5 mm brim fits by the measured envelope. Check all actual brim/support,
   skirt and purge/prime structures in the slice preview; extra structures can
   use the small side margin. Keep the P1S profile's normal exclusion zones.
5. Confirm the complete slice stays in the usable area before making a job.
   Resolve any warning in Studio without shrinking, silently splitting or
   changing the printer's physical limits. Keep wire channels and air slots
   clear of support residue.

CAMERA / SELFIE REQUIREMENT
The selected R4 keeps the original Korvo camera position and its front opening.
Do not cover the camera with leather, adhesive, grille material or an added
opaque face skin. Read Camera-and-selfies.txt and check the live view with the
actual board fitted to TEST06, then repeat with the complete first shell.
