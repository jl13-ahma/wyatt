28 October2026 — R2 adaptation for this Wyatt project:
Added detachable ventilated SEN0428 sensor pod, removable lid, adjustable PCB-edge
shoes, remote desk foot, sensor fit coupon and open harness/strain-relief routing.
Extended saddle sensor platform; added conservative screw-head clearances and
full-thickness lower-retainer/rear-cover reliefs. Retained the unchanged official
inner-case source files, original attribution and CC BY-SA4.0 terms. New geometry
and geometry source in this R2 folder use those same CC BY-SA4.0 terms. Sensor
manufacturer text dimensions are referenced by URL; unverified component/header
coordinates were not copied or invented. This is untested print-prototype CAD.

2026-10-28 R3 adaptation: removed R2 external sensor pod, remote foot and wing
platform; restored compact154mm saddle while retaining R2retainer/head clearances.
Added lower-centre integrated ambient cell, front/bottom vents, forward-exiting
saddle air tunnel, edge carrier with positive end fences, rear retention frame,
inset rear service cap, supported silicone gasket land, closed roofed wire-seal
feedthrough, hidden rear chase/cover and matching retainer relief, and real-cell
fit coupon. New geometry, native source and derived drawings remain CC BY-SA4.0.
No original Espressif geometry or license text was changed.

28 October2026 — R4 horizontal adaptation:
Rebuilt a single continuous horizontal cabinet with speakers to the left/right of
the unchanged official cassette, hollow wing walls/ribs, flush rectangular grilles,
one top/side cognac material reference, supported outer roof and rear-open service
lanes. Added low two-pad plinth, relocated isolated internal sensor cell, supported
foam seals, under-grille divider, closed feedthrough, full bottom duct and full-depth
port-access coupon. Removed R3 stacked cabinet geometry from this revision.
New/derived geometry, source and technical drawings retain CC BY-SA4.0. Official
Espressif original geometry and license files remain unchanged.
