WYATT R4 / INTERNAL ROOM-SENSOR FIT AND THERMAL CHECK
28 October 2026. Design and proposed test plan; no physical measurements made.

CURRENT PLACEMENT
R4 uses one horizontal body with the screen in the centre and one speaker on
each side. The SEN0428 sits inside a separate ambient cell below the left
speaker. The current CAD manifest and assembly notes define its actual position,
grille divider, rear seal, wire feedthrough and support keepouts. Earlier R3
lower-centre geometry and R2 external-pod geometry are superseded for this build.

The supplied SEN0428 outline is 14 x 17 mm. PCB thickness, assembled height,
component/inlet locations, blank edge lands and cable/header stack need the real
module. Keep the low-profile pigtail, positive edge carrier and physical coupon.
No board-hole coordinates or assembled module envelope have been invented.

FIT AND AIR PATH
Trial the actual sensor, carrier, retention, cap, screws and soft seals together
on the new R4 sensor-cell coupon. The sensing inlet and components must stay
clear; only inspected blank PCB edges may contact the carrier. Use a removable
edge shim if needed; the cap must not flatten a tall header or load the chip.
Preserve the independent ambient slots, left-grille divider and rear seal.
The sensor must not share a hidden under-grille air gap with the driver. Check
the divider/seal contact on the actual print. Valid CAD is not an air-leak test.
Keep leather, pads, support residue, desk mats and wires out of every vent path.
Thread free pigtail ends through the closed channel before fitting a larger
connector. Leave a service loop; verify the actual inner-case connector entry.
Finish and fully cure printed/coated/leather-glued parts before sensor fitting.
Do not coat, glue or wash the sensing inlet with alcohol/acetone or other solvent.

SAME-PROBE A-B-A COMPARISON
Use raw readings with the heater off and identical polling. Record time, display
brightness, Wi-Fi activity, audio level and camera mode. A nearby ambient
reference helps identify room drift; outside forecasts are not a room reference.

A: temporarily position the same probe outside the powered case, away from
hand/body heat, board exhaust, direct sun and drafts. A roughly 20 cm separation
is a test arrangement, not a manufacturer rule. Record a stable 10 minute baseline.
B: install it in the fully closed R4 case in its normal desk position. Run at
least 30 minutes, then observe 10 stable minutes. A provisional plateau rule is
less than 0.05 C/min change; flag a sample still drifting after 60 minutes.
A: remove it using the service cap and loop, then repeat the outside baseline.
Repeat a run if room/reference drift is material (e.g. over 0.2 C between the
bracketing baselines). Compare installed values against a drift-adjusted A-A
baseline instead of a second uncorrected probe.

Repeat with relevant loads: bright screen, active Wi-Fi, local voice/alarm
playback, longer left/right speaker use and camera if used. The nearby left
driver deserves its own heat comparison. Use local audio/traffic for this screen;
paid AI requests are unnecessary. Keep all final leather, seals and pads fitted.

Proposed prototype screening targets: added installed bias at most 0.5 C and
3 RH percentage points in each mode, and mode-to-mode added-bias change at most
0.3 C. These are chosen engineering targets, not advertised device accuracy or
manufacturer limits. Investigate slower response (e.g. over twice the detached
response time) during a modest dry-room temperature transition without water or
condensation exposure. Revise airflow, isolation or placement if needed.
A guessed constant temperature offset cannot correct load-dependent bias or RH.

No room sensor is fitted today; no installed accuracy follows from these files.
Keep the existing SEN0428, safe shared 3.3 V I2C harness and current 0x44/AD1B
firmware expectation. A new controller, display, amplifier or speaker pair is
unnecessary. Exact hardware and soft-piece quantities come from the R4 manifest.
