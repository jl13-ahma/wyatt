FORM AND FILES
One continuous rounded block surrounds the central official cassette and two
front-facing speakers. Two flush44×86mm perforated panels conceal the round
speaker apertures. A single cognac material reference wraps the top and sides;
there are no stacked speaker/display boxes. The room sensor is internal.

Nominal main printed block240W×112.8H×35.2D. The low plinth is3.2mm tall, with
concealed rear pads and a narrow connecting bar, within the main width. Printed
assembly240×116×60. With the illustrative0.85mm leather/adhesive reference,
241.7×116.85×60. Rear pan-head screws may project2.8mm locally; they remain
inside the rear-foot depth. Leather is applied after measuring the actual print,
not an invented moulded texture. It is excluded from print meshes.

Espressif's unchanged official110.9×97.8×23.6 inner-case reference is centred in
the opening. Camera, microphones, front buttons, source port openings and the

working device claim. The default public preview uses a neutral display.

ASSEMBLY ORDER / HARDWARE
Finish, fully cure and air printed/coated/leather-glued parts BEFORE installing
the sensor. Sensirion flags adhesive/plasticizer VOCs and cleaners as contaminants.
No glue, paint, IPA or acetone on the sensor. No fabric/foam across its inlet.
Trial the actual cassette/ports, both speakers and sensor coupons first. Install
speakers with individually fitted stationary-frame stops, route loose leads,
seat the official case, fit04retainer, then thread/fit sensor wiring and foam.
Install08/09carrier on four small seats, soft gasket and07cap. Fit rear plates,
front contact frames/panels and low plinth. Verify no wire/cone contact and no
closed ventilation path before power. Actual connector entry must already pass.

PHYSICAL ACCEPTANCE
Check actual cone excursion, cable strain, all port/control access and reversible
sensor service. On a flat desk trial the finished unit's normal touch force and
gentle cable tug without tipping/sliding; no tip or impact rating is claimed.
For room readings, keep heater off and record raw data: same-sensor detachedA→
installedB→detachedA, minimum30min and10min stable observations, in idle and
highbrightness/WiFi/audio/camera modes. Suggested prototype screening targets:
added bias≤0.5°C/3RHpercentagepoints, mode drift≤0.3°C. These are engineering
targets, not manufacturer/system-accuracy claims. Revise isolation/airflow before
using guessed offsets or making room-temperature claims if the target fails.
