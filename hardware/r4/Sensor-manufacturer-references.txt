ENCLOSURE / THERMAL REQUIREMENTS
Sensirion recommends a small sensor air volume exposed to ambient, separate
from the warm electronics compartment, low thermal coupling and useful airflow
through multiple openings. Keep the sensor away from display/SoC/regulator heat
and their rising warm exhaust. A separate ventilated pod is a reasonable design
implementation; exact gap/vent millimetres are engineering allowances requiring
thermal testing, not manufacturer dimensions. Keep the sensor's top opening
clear; support/clamp the PCB edges, not the chip. Do not block vents with leather.
Mounting increases thermal mass/response time even with a good opening. The
component's accuracy specification is not a whole-enclosure accuracy guarantee.
https://sensirion.com/media/documents/FC5BED84/662B494D/Sensirion_Humidity_Temperature_Design_Guide.pdf
https://github.com/Sensirion/smartgadget-sht4x-housing

Glues/adhesives/plasticizers may outgas and contaminate the sensing polymer;
solvents including alcohols/acetone and direct cleaning can also cause issues.
Prefer mechanical retention in the sensor pod. Cure/air printed, coated and
leather-glued parts before installing the sensor; keep adhesive outside its
cavity and do not wash/coat/glue the sensing chip. No IP/waterproof rating is
established for the proposed printed pod.
https://sensirion.com/media/documents/D1297BA5/670547C3/HT_AN_Contamination_Guide.pdf
